Balewadi

Rohan Ekam stands on the banks of the Mula River in Balewadi. One of its ground floors is 14 floors above ground, in the sky. Complete with trees, park benches, and play areas - everything you would expect to find on the ground floor. There is, of course, a ground floor on the ground floor as well. With the choice of 2, 3, and 4-bedroom homes across 9 blocks, homes at Rohan Ekam are all built to Rohan’s PLUS design philosophy —perfect ventilation, lively light, utmost privacy, and smartly designed spaces. Rohan’s rigorous construction standards spill over into the rest of the complex too. Design that is intuitive, accessible, and clean is evident everywhere - from the clubhouse and the indoor sports complex right down to the common areas and the parking system.

Project Overview

+

Is the project RERA compliant?

+

Yes, Rohan Ekam is RERA registered. The MahaRERA Registration Number is P52100052298 and all information can be accessed on the MahaRERA portal.

Is the project under PMC or PMRDA?

+

Rohan Ekam comes into the limits of the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).

What is the possession date for Rohan Ekam?

+

As per RERA, the possession date for Rohan Ekam Phase 1 is December 2027.

Is there any ongoing litigation or legal disputes associated with the project?

+

The project has received approval and a go ahead to proceed from a solicitor firm.

When will we get the completion certificate?

+

Typically, the completion certificate is issued by the relevant local authority after the construction is finished and has undergone the necessary inspections and approvals.

When is the agreement process expected to commence for the project?

+

The agreement process will commence after the token collection phase. Once the token collection is completed, the allocation and agreement process will follow.

Is this possession date guaranteed?

+

We are committed to deadlines. Because we were originally contractors, we pride ourselves on timely delivery considering the stakes are high for the client if delivery is delayed. What is the likelihood of Rohan Builders completing the project ‘Rohan Ekam’ on time?

Rohan Builders has a strong track record of timely project completion, backed by its CRISIL DA2+ rating for consistent quality and delivery. With over three decades of experience and a dedicated in-house engineering team, Rohan Ekam is progressing as per planned timelines.

Location & Connectivity

+

Where is Rohan Ekam located in Pune?

+

Rohan Ekam is strategically located at the heart of Balewadi in West Pune. It is very close to the Pune-Mumbai Expressway and is easily accessible from Hinjewadi, Baner, Aundh Wakad, Pashan.

How is Rohan Ekam accessible – road, rail or metro?

+

Rohan Ekam, located in Balewadi, Pune, enjoys good connectivity to various parts of the city. Balewadi is well-connected to major roads and highways, including the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and Pune-Bengaluru Highway (NH-4). The Pune Mahanagar Parivahan Mahamandal Limited (PMPML) provides bus services in the area, making it convenient to commute within Pune. Balewadi is also served by auto-rickshaws and cab services. Pune International Airport and Pune Railway Station are within few kms.

Design, Features & Specifications

+

What are the configurations and carpet areas of the units?

+

Rohan Ekam has been designed for all. It has different unit configurations, carpet areas and sizes as below: 2BHK – 870-890 sq.ft., 3BHK – 1046-1500 sq.ft., 4BHK – 1842-2160 sq.ft.

What is PLUS Home?

+

PLUS stands for Perfect Ventilation, Lively Light, Utmost Privacy, and Smartly Designed Spaces. It is Rohan’s design philosophy that ensures every home is thoughtfully engineered for comfort, functionality, and natural flow.

Will the specifications in the mock-up flat be consistent with those in the actual units?

+

We are offering two types of specifications: Type-1 (standard) and Type-2 (enhanced), allowing homebuyers to choose according to their preferences and needs.

Is there a provision for solar water in the bathroom?

+

No. However, a provision will provided in the sink

What are the internal amenities offered?

+

Homes at Rohan Ekam come with premium specifications including vitrified flooring, granite kitchen platform with sink, laminated main door, powder-coated aluminium windows with mosquito mesh, premium sanitary fittings, concealed wiring with modular switches, and video door phone facility. Select units feature enhanced finishes such as marble flooring, wooden flooring in the master bedroom, and a digital lock on the main door.

What construction technology is used?

+

The construction technology used for this project is Alu Form technology. This innovative construction method allows for rapid construction compared to traditional methods. The use of aluminum formwork ensures consistency in structural elements, resulting in durable and well-finished buildings.

Are all units Vastu-compliant?

+

We try to match your Vastu requirements with the available unit options.

If customers choose the enhanced specifications, how much additional cost will they need to pay?

+

Customers opting for the enhanced specifications will incur an additional cost of approximately Rs. 500 per square foot.

Will the railings have toughened glass?

+

Yes! The inclusion of toughened glass in the railings will provide an added layer of safety and security.

How tall is the building?

+

There are a total of 34 floors, out of which 28 floors have been launched.

What is the clear ceiling height within each unit?

+

The clear ceiling height within each apartment is approximately 9 feet.

Does Rohan Ekam equip with Home-Automation features?

+

Select apartments at Rohan Ekam include provisions for digital door locks and home-automation readiness, allowing residents to integrate smart home devices as per their preference.

Will you be providing an additional sink in the utility area, as shown in the mock-up flat?

+

Yes, an additional sink will be provided in the utility area, consistent with what was shown in the mock-up flat. The additional utility sink will enhance convenience for tasks like cleaning and dishwashing, making daily chores more efficient and manageable.

Will shower cubicle be included in the washrooms?

+

The provision of a glass partition for shower cubicles will be available as part of the enhanced specifications only.

Will mosquito mesh be installed on the windows and sliders?

+

Yes! Mosquito mesh will be provided on the windows and sliders of the units, enhancing comfort and protection from insects.

Will there be light points provided in the terrace or balcony areas?

+

Light points will be provided in the terrace/balcony areas of the units, enhancing the usability and ambiance of these spaces.

Infrastructure & Services

+

How is the water supply taken care of?

+

Our water supply is sourced from PMC (Pune Municipal Corporation), and we are proud to implement rainwater harvesting and sewage water treatment systems to manage water resources efficiently.

Is there a generator backup in the apartment during power outages?

+

100% backup for common amenities is made available. Each room in the apartment is equipped with backup power for one light point and one fan point to ensure uninterrupted electricity supply.

Is there a piped natural gas (PNG) connection available within the project?

+

It will be MNGL (Maharashtra Natural Gas Limited) who will handle the provision of the piped natural gas connection whenever they decide to provide it.

What security measures or precautions have been implemented in the project to address the risk of flooding or manage flood-related situations?

+

The project plans have received approval from development authorities, and its elevation, approximately 25 feet above the river level, should help mitigate flood-related concerns. This proactive approach assures safety and compliance with regulatory requirements.

What are the safety & security measures at Rohan Ekam?

+

Rohan Ekam is equipped with CCTV surveillance in select common areas, video door phones in each apartment, controlled access points, and fire-fighting systems. Sanitation facilities for staff and round-the-clock security ensure residents’ safety and well-being.

Are there any borewells within the project?

+

Yes, there are borewells located within the project premises.

What about the electricity/power supply?

+

Electricity is supplied through the MSEDCL (Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited).

Where is the Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) located within the project?

+

The Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) is strategically positioned near the entrance of the project to maintain an open and spacious atmosphere within the project premises. This placement also serves the purpose of directing any potential odors away from the residential area, contributing to a more pleasant living environment inside.

What are the sustainability aspects of the project?

+

Rohan Ekam integrates sustainability through solar-powered lighting in common areas, sewage treatment plant, organic waste composting, and use of native plant species to encourage biodiversity.

Legal, Approvals & Maintenance

+

How are the maintenance charges calculated?

+

The calculation for the maintenance fees is: Carpet X 8 X 12.

Who will be responsible for the maintenance, and until when will the developer be in charge of it?

+

The developer will oversee maintenance for the first year. Following this period, a society will be formed. If the society chooses to retain the same agencies, the builder will facilitate the transfer of these agencies to the society’s management. The agencies are under the care of the property manager.

When is the society formed?

+

The society is formed after 1 year.

Is the maintenance fee a one-time payment or is it recurring?

+

The maintenance fee is applicable for only one year; after this period, we will establish the society.
