## 🏛️ Project Overview
**Rohan Ekam** is a 13-acre ultra-luxury riverfront residential landmark located along the scenic banks of the **Mula River** in **Balewadi, Pune**, adjacent to Balewadi High Street.

This digital experience application serves as an interactive monograph and luxury portal showcasing the project's pioneering architectural breakthroughs, physics-engineered living, and world-class amenities.

---

## ✨ Key Architectural Highlights & Experiences

### 1. ☁️ "The Ground Floor in the Sky" (Level 14)
- An unprecedented **14th-floor suspended landscape realm** positioned 150 feet in the air.
- Inter-tower sky bridges linking the residential towers into an integrated vertical neighborhood.
- Open-air cloud co-working pods, suspended reading gazebos, elevated yoga lawn, and Sahyadri sunset viewing deck.

### 2. 🔬 Interactive "Rohan PLUS" Physics Simulator
Interactive laboratory allowing visitors to test Rohan's 30-year design philosophy:
- **P (Perfect Ventilation):** Real-time wind speed & direction slider displaying air changes per hour (ACH) through cross-ventilated living suites.
- **L (Lively Light):** Solar time-of-day slider (6:00 AM to 7:00 PM) showing sun azimuth, ambient lux levels, and overhang shading.
- **U (Utmost Privacy):** Staggered 45° balcony angle simulator demonstrating 0% direct visual overlap.
- **S (Smart Space):** Usable carpet comparison slider (Rohan 0% dead-space vs typical 18% wasted hallway apartments).

### 3. 📐 Interactive Residences & Floor Plan Studio
- **2 BHK Opulence** (869 – 987 sq.ft carpet)
- **3 BHK Grande** (1,046 – 1,410 sq.ft carpet)
- **4 BHK Royal Sanctuary with Maid Suite** (1,840 – 2,158 sq.ft carpet)
- **Sky Penthouse Duplex** (2,650 – 3,200 sq.ft carpet)
- Clickable CAD floor plan schematic with dimension breakdowns, daylight coefficients, and luxury specifications.

### 4. 🧭 Balewadi 360° Neighborhood Radar
- Live distance and commute timer to:
  - Balewadi High Street (4 mins)
  - Pune Metro Line 3 Station (4 mins)
  - Mumbai-Pune Expressway (8 mins)
  - Hinjawadi IT Park Phase 1 (12 mins)
  - Baner Commercial Hub & Amar Paradigm (7 mins)
  - Jupiter Hospital & Manipal Hospital (8-9 mins)

### 5. 💰 Smart Financial Suite & EMI Calculator
- Real-time loan calculator with sliders for Property Value, Down Payment, Interest Rate, and Tenure.
- Visual principal vs. interest breakdown chart.
- Complete statutory cost estimation (Maha Stamp Duty 7%, GST 5%, Registration).

### 6. 🌿 Procedural River Breeze & Nature Soundscape
- Built-in **Web Audio API generator** synthesizing soothing organic river breezes and gentle bird chirps directly in the browser with zero external MP3 downloads.

### 7. 🎟️ VIP Concierge & Tour Scheduler
- Interactive private tour booking with chauffeur pickup request and instant WhatsApp concierge connect.
