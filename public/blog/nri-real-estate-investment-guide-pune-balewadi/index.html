NRI Real Estate Investment Guide: Why West Pune is the Top Choice for Global Buyers

By Rohan Ekam Market Research Desk • Published: November 2026 • 8 min read • Location: Balewadi, Pune

With the Indian rupee's competitive exchange rate and Pune's emergence as India's foremost technology and manufacturing hub, Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are directing significant capital into West Pune's luxury real estate corridors. Among micro-markets, Balewadi stands out as the premier destination for capital safety, high rental yields, and institutional-grade transparency.

1. Why Pune Outperforms Mumbai & Bengaluru for NRI Investors

  • Rational Capital Values: Prime luxury properties in Balewadi (such as Rohan Ekam) command ₹10,500 – ₹13,000 per sq. ft., compared to ₹35,000+ in Mumbai suburbs or ₹16,000+ in East Bengaluru, offering far greater capital upside.
  • Stable Tech Tenant Base: Proximity to Hinjawadi IT Park (Phase 1, 2 & 3) ensures low vacancy rates. Expat managers, enterprise tech directors, and senior consultants actively seek luxury riverfront residences with international-standard amenities.
  • Institutional Governance (MahaRERA): Maharashtra's regulatory authority is India's strictest real estate regulator. 2. Regulatory & Banking Framework for NRIs (RBI Guidelines)

Under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), NRIs and OCIs do not require prior permission from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to acquire residential property in India.

Quick Buyer FAQ

Can NRIs repatriate sale proceeds from Balewadi apartments abroad?

Yes. When purchased via NRE or FCNR inward banking channels under FEMA regulations, NRIs and OCIs may repatriate principal funds up to two residential properties subject to Form 15CA/15CB.

Can an NRI complete Rohan Ekam registration without flying to Pune?

Yes. NRIs can execute a consularized Special Power of Attorney (PoA) abroad and adjudicate it in Pune within 90 days for registration.
