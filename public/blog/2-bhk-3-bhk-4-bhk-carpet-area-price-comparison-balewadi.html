2. Understanding Statutory Taxes & Registration (Maharashtra)

When reviewing all-inclusive cost sheets for properties in Pune, buyers must account for government levies:

  • GST (Goods and Services Tax): 5% on under-construction non-affordable residential units.
  • Stamp Duty: 7% in Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) limits (includes 5% Base Stamp Duty + 1% Local Body Tax / LBT + 1% Metro Cess).
  • Registration Fees: 1% of property value (capped at ₹30,000 for residential properties).
