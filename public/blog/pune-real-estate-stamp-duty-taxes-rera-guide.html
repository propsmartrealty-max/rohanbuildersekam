Pune Real Estate Stamp Duty, Taxes & MahaRERA Guide 2026-2027

A definitive handbook for Pune home buyers and investors. Everything you need to know about Maharashtra stamp duty rates, 1% Metro Cess, ₹30,000 registration fee caps, 5% GST, income tax deductions, and step-by-step MahaRERA verification.

By Rohan Ekam Legal & Financial Advisory • Published November 2026 • 9 min read • RERA Regulatory Guide

Navigating Pune Property Acquisition Costs

When investing in luxury real estate in Pune—such as Rohan Ekam in Balewadi—calculating your total financial commitment requires accounting for statutory government taxes and regulatory fees beyond the base agreement value.

Maharashtra's real estate taxation framework is among India's most transparent, governed by the Maharashtra Stamp Act, the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act (RERA), and the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council. Below is a comprehensive breakdown of statutory rates in effect for 2026-2027.

1. Maharashtra Stamp Duty & Registration Fee Structure (2026-2027)

Stamp duty in Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) and Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC) jurisdictions is composed of two components: the base state stamp duty plus the 1% Metro Cess / Local Body Tax (LBT) implemented to fund urban mass transit systems like the Pune Metro.

Buyer Category Base Stamp Duty Metro Cess / LBT Total Effective Stamp Duty
Male Purchaser (Sole Owner) 5.0% 1.0% 6.0% of Agreement Value

Corporate Entity / LLP 5.0% 1.0% 6.0% of Agreement Value
Registration Fee Cap: Unlike stamp duty, Maharashtra property registration charges are strictly capped at ₹30,000 for all residential properties with an agreement value exceeding ₹30 Lakhs. For properties below ₹30 Lakhs, the registration fee is 1% of the agreement value.

2. Goods and Services Tax (GST) on Under-Construction Properties

Under the revised GST regime for Indian residential real estate:

  • Non-Affordable / Luxury Housing: Residential units having a carpet area above 60 sq.m. (approx. 645 sq.ft.) in metro cities or with an agreement value exceeding ₹45 Lakhs are taxed at a flat 5% GST (without Input Tax Credit). All 2 BHK, 3 BHK, and 4 BHK residences at Rohan Ekam fall into this premier category.
  • Completed / Ready-to-Move Units: Properties that have received an official Occupancy Certificate (OC) or Completion Certificate from the local municipal planning authority attract 0% GST.

Before executing any legal agreement or transferring booking tokens, every purchaser must verify the project credentials on the official Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority portal (maharera.mahaonline.gov.in):

  1. Check MahaRERA Registration Numbers: Ensure the project has an active sanction certificate. Rohan Ekam holds two distinct registrations:

  3. Inspect Approved Sanction Plans: Review the uploaded architectural layout, floor plates, elevation drawings, environmental clearances, and commencement certificates (CC).

4. Income Tax Deductions for Pune Home Buyers (FY 2026-2027)

Purchasing residential property with a home loan unlocks substantial annual income tax deductions under the Indian Income Tax Act:

  • Section 80C (Principal Repayment): Deduct up to ₹1,50,000 annually against the principal portion of your home loan repayment. Note that stamp duty and registration charges paid during the financial year of purchase can also be claimed under the Section 80C basket.
  • Section 24(b) (Interest Repayment): For a self-occupied property, deduct up to ₹2,00,000 annually against the interest paid on your home loan. For rented-out properties, the entire interest amount paid can be set off against rental income, subject to standard set-off caps.
  • Section 54 (Capital Gains Reinvestment): Long-term capital gains earned from the sale of another property can be completely shielded from tax if re-invested into a new residential home like Rohan Ekam within statutory timeframes.
