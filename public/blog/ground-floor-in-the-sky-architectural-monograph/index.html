The Ground Floor in the Sky: Reimagining High-Rise Community Life at Level 14

By Rohan Ekam Market Research Desk • Published: November 2026 • 8 min read • Location: Balewadi, Pune

For over a century, high-rise living has wrestled with an inherent paradox: as towers ascend to capture light and panoramic horizons, they sever the resident's intimate connection to earth, nature, and community life. In most residential developments, the ground floor is crowded with vehicular traffic, security checkpoints, and parking drop-offs.

1. The Vision of Ar. Sanjay Mohe (Mindspace Architects)

Recognized as one of India's preeminent master architects, Ar. Sanjay Mohe formulated a radical solution for Rohan Ekam's 13-acre riverside canvas: What if we took the ground floor — with its sprawling lawns, shaded pavilions, open courtyards, and communal vibrancy — and elevated it into the sky?

The result is The Ground Floor in the Sky at Level 14, a continuous elevated promenade connecting the towers horizontally, suspended 140 feet above the Mula River.

2. Structural Anatomy of the Sky Expanse

  • Engineered Sky Bridges: Specialized seismic-isolated expansion joints connect adjacent residential towers, creating a continuous pedestrian deck where residents can stroll between buildings without touching the ground.
  • Cascading Sloping Lawns: Real turf lawns engineered with lightweight aerated soil substrates, drainage membranes, and drip irrigation, bringing the feel of a grounded villa lawn to high-altitude living.
  • Co-Working Glass Pods: High-speed acoustic glass enclosures equipped with gigabit fiber, power ports, and panoramic river perspectives for remote executives and founders.
  • High-Altitude Yoga & Meditation Decks: Positioned east-west to capture dawn river reflections and sunset breezes over the Sahyadri foothills.

3. Wind-Tunnel Engineering and Micro-Climate Simulation

Constructing a continuous outdoor community expanse at Level 14 requires solving intense atmospheric winds. The design of Rohan Ekam underwent extensive boundary-layer wind tunnel testing to position wind-baffle pergolas and acoustic glass screens. These aerodynamic elements slow down gusting river breezes into gentle, continuous airflows, ensuring comfort throughout every season.
