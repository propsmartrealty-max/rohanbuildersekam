Why Buying a Flat in Baner Represents Pune's Safest & Highest ROI Investment

Baner is celebrated as Western Pune's most mature and coveted residential enclave. Renowned for its cosmopolitan culture, tree-lined avenues, elite lifestyle clubs, and vibrant commercial high streets, Baner continues to attract Pune's top professionals, business families, and global tech leaders. However, dense inland urbanisation has made expansive green open spaces rare in Baner.

Rohan Ekam offers the perfect antidote: situated along the pristine Mula Riverfront directly connecting Baner and Balewadi, it provides a 13-acre lush green retreat with over 75% open landscaped ground, while keeping you just moments away from Baner Road, Balewadi High Street, and Westend Mall Aundh.

Crafted with Rohan PLUS Homes Philosophy

Every home at Rohan Ekam is engineered according to the acclaimed Rohan PLUS Design Philosophy:

  • Perfect Ventilation: Wind-tunnel tested dual-corridor layouts ensuring natural cross-breezes flowing off the Mula River.
  • Lively Light: Floor-to-ceiling glass fenestrations illuminating every corner of the residence from sunrise to sunset.
  • Utmost Privacy: Masterfully angled towers ensuring zero window intrusion from facing apartments.
  • Smart Space: 100% usable carpet area with zero internal corridor wastage.

Coupled with The Ground Floor in the Sky at Level 14, residents experience resort-scale community life 140 feet above the ground.
