The Waterfront Advantage: Why Riverfront Properties Command a 30% Premium

Across major global gateway cities and India’s prime urban centers, waterfront real estate consistently outperforms all inland residential categories. In Pune, true waterfront land parcels are virtually impossible to assemble due to strict environmental zoning laws and fragmented urban land ownership.
