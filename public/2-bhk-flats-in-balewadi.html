The Rohan PLUS Engineering in 2 BHK Layouts

In conventional 2 BHK flats across Balewadi, up to 18% of paid square footage is lost to dark internal passages and awkward structural corners. At Rohan Ekam, master architect Ar. Sanjay Mohe implemented the Rohan PLUS Architecture:

  • P - Perfect Ventilation: Dual-aspect window placements induce continuous cross-breezes from the Mula River basin, significantly cutting air conditioning loads.
  • L - Lively Light: Floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass panels ensure natural sunlight reaches the innermost living and cooking zones.
  • U - Utmost Privacy: Zero opposite window views. Your windows look out onto river expanses or cascading lawns, never into a neighbor's room.
  • S - Smart Space: No dead corridors. Every single square foot serves functional living, giving a 2 BHK the spacious feel of a standard 3 BHK.
