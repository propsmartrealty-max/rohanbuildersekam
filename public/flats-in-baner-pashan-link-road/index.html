Baner-Pashan Link Road Real Estate: Space vs. Density

The Baner-Pashan Link Road (BPLR) is historically celebrated as one of Baner's most verdant and tranquil residential pockets, offering seamless connectivity to Pashan Lake, Bavdhan, and Kothrud. However, rapid commercialization and land parcel scarcity have resulted in standalone residential towers built on cramped 1- to 2-acre plots, leaving very little room for expansive sports amenities or open landscape greens.

Located just 6 minutes away across Baner Road, Rohan Ekam offers the expansive scale that BPLR developments can no longer provide. Situated on a continuous 13-acre riverside parcel, Rohan Ekam delivers an uncrowded lifestyle with 9 boutique towers, an Olympic-length lap pool, and a 1.2 km riverside boardwalk promenade.
